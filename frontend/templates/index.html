A more connected campus

Campus Lost & Found is a central place to report missing belongings and reunite found items with their owners. We built it to replace scattered messages and notice boards with a clearer, more reliable process for students and campus security.

Students can submit a lost-item report and follow its status. Security staff register items handed in, review possible matches, and verify ownership before recording a return—helping the whole campus community get back to what matters.
